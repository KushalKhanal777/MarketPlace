Our Story

Founded in 2026, Islington Marketplace was born from the vision of creating a world-class e-commerce platform for Nepal. What started as a small team with big dreams has grown into a thriving marketplace connecting thousands of buyers and sellers across the country.

We are proud to support local businesses and entrepreneurs, giving them the tools and platform they need to reach customers nationwide. From electronics to fashion, home goods to beauty products, Islington Marketplace is your one-stop shop for everything you need.
