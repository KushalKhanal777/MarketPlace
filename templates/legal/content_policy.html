Policies

Content Policy

Guidelines for creating and managing content on Islington Marketplace

Product Images

Product images are the first impression buyers have of your items. Follow these standards:

  • Resolution: Minimum 800x800px. Recommended: 1200x1200px or higher
  • Format: JPG or PNG. WebP accepted but JPG/PNG preferred
  • Background: Clean, white or neutral background. Lifestyle shots encouraged as secondary images
  • Lighting: Well-lit with accurate color representation. No heavy filters or editing
  • Multiple views: Include front, back, side, and detail shots (minimum 3 images per listing)
  • No watermarks: Images must not contain text overlays, logos, or watermarks
  • Accurate representation: Images must accurately depict the actual product, including color, size, and condition

Do: Use natural lighting on a clean white background showing the actual product

Don't: Use stock photos, heavily filtered images, or photos from other sellers

Product Descriptions

Accurate descriptions reduce returns and build buyer trust:

  • Be specific: Include dimensions, weight, materials, color, and key features
  • Be honest: Disclose any defects, wear, or limitations of the product
  • Condition: Clearly state whether the item is new, like-new, used, or refurbished
  • Compatibility: If applicable, list compatible devices, brands, or accessories
  • What's included: Specify exactly what the buyer will receive (product, accessories, manuals, etc.)
  • No false claims: Do not make exaggerated or unverifiable claims about product benefits

Do: "Cotton t-shirt, 100% organic cotton, machine washable. Available in S, M, L. Color may vary slightly from photos."

Don't: "BEST SHIRT EVER!!! Will change your life! Guaranteed 100% perfect forever!"

Reviews & Ratings

Honest reviews are the foundation of our marketplace trust system:

  • Authenticity: Only genuine buyers should leave reviews for products they purchased
  • Honest feedback: Share your real experience — both positive and constructive
  • No fake reviews: Sellers must not create fake reviews, pay for reviews, or offer incentives for positive ratings
  • No review manipulation: Do not ask buyers to remove or change their reviews in exchange for favors
  • Photo reviews: Encouraged! Photo reviews help other buyers make informed decisions
  • Respectful language: Reviews should be respectful and relevant to the product

Prohibited Content

The following types of content are not permitted anywhere on the platform:

  • Hate speech: Content that promotes discrimination, violence, or hatred against individuals or groups
  • Harassment: Bullying, threats, or abusive behavior toward other users
  • Misleading information: False claims, fake discounts, or deceptive pricing tactics
  • Spam: Repetitive, unsolicited, or automated content
  • Personal information: Sharing others' personal data without consent
  • Illegal content: Any content that violates Nepalese law or international regulations
  • Malicious links: Phishing attempts, malware, or deceptive URLs

Reporting Violations

Help us maintain a safe marketplace by reporting content that violates these guidelines:

  • How to report: Click the "Report" button on any product listing, review, or seller profile
  • Provide details: Explain why the content violates our policies. Include evidence when possible
  • Categories: Select the most relevant reason — fraud, counterfeit, inappropriate content, age-restricted, prohibited item, misleading description, harassment, or spam
  • Confidentiality: Reporter identity is kept confidential from the reported party
  • No retaliation: Filing good-faith reports will never result in account penalties

Enforcement

We take content violations seriously. Our enforcement actions include:

  • Content removal: Violating content will be removed promptly upon detection or report
  • Content editing: Minor violations may result in content being edited to comply with guidelines
  • Listing suppression: Non-compliant listings may be hidden from search results
  • Account warnings: First-time or minor violations receive a written warning
  • Temporary suspension: Repeated violations result in 7-30 day account suspension
  • Permanent ban: Severe or repeated violations lead to permanent account termination
  • Payout hold: Accounts under review may have pending payouts held until resolution

Our moderation team reviews all reports within 48 hours. Sellers may appeal enforcement decisions through the support team.
