Seller Guidelines

Everything you need to know to succeed on Islington Marketplace

Getting Started

Welcome to Islington Marketplace! Here's what you need to know to begin selling:

  • Complete your profile: Ensure your seller profile is fully filled out with accurate business information and a professional profile image
  • Verify your identity: Complete the KYC verification process by uploading valid government-issued identification
  • Set up payment: Configure your preferred payout method (eSewa, Khalti, or bank transfer) in your dashboard
  • Understand fees: Familiarize yourself with our commission structure (typically 10% per sale)
  • Review policies: Read all seller guidelines, prohibited items list, and content policies before listing

Product Listing Standards

High-quality listings lead to more sales. Follow these standards:

  • Product images: Use clear, well-lit photos on a clean background. Include multiple angles. Minimum resolution: 800x800px
  • No watermarks: Product images should not contain watermarks, logos, or text overlays
  • Accurate descriptions: Provide detailed, honest descriptions including dimensions, materials, features, and any defects
  • Correct categorization: Place products in the most appropriate category. Miscategorized products may be removed
  • Pricing: All prices must be in Nepalese Rupees (NPR). Clearly indicate if shipping is included
  • Stock levels: Keep inventory counts accurate. Overselling leads to order cancellations and penalties
  • Brand authenticity: Only list genuine products. Counterfeit items will result in immediate account suspension

Pricing Policy

Fair and transparent pricing is essential for buyer trust:

  • Prices must be reasonable and competitive for the market
  • Do not artificially inflate prices to show fake "discounts"
  • All applicable taxes (VAT) should be included or clearly stated
  • Shipping costs must be disclosed before checkout
  • Price changes cannot be applied to already-placed orders

Pro Tip: Products with competitive pricing and free shipping tend to sell 40% faster on our platform.

Shipping Requirements

Reliable shipping is critical for customer satisfaction:

  • Processing time: Orders must be shipped within 2 business days of confirmation
  • Packaging: Use appropriate packaging to prevent damage during transit
  • Tracking: Provide tracking information for all shipments
  • Free shipping: Consider offering free shipping on orders over Rs. 5,000 (our platform standard)
  • Insurance: Recommended for high-value items (over Rs. 10,000)
  • Local delivery: For Kathmandu Valley, aim for same-day or next-day delivery when possible

Return Policy

We maintain a buyer-friendly return policy to build trust:

  • 30-day returns: Buyers may return items within 30 days of delivery for any reason
  • Return conditions: Items must be unused, in original packaging, with tags attached
  • Defective items: Sellers bear return shipping costs for defective or misrepresented items
  • Refund timeline: Refunds must be processed within 5 business days of receiving the return
  • Disputes: If you disagree with a return request, you may escalate through the platform's dispute resolution

Seller Ratings

Your seller rating directly impacts your visibility and sales:

  • Rating system: Buyers rate sellers on a 1-5 star scale based on product quality, shipping speed, and communication
  • Performance metrics: We track order completion rate, on-time shipping rate, and customer satisfaction
  • Top Seller badge: Maintaining a 4.5+ rating with 50+ sales unlocks the Top Seller badge
  • Rating impact: Low ratings (below 3.5) may result in reduced visibility or account review
  • Fake reviews: Posting fake reviews or incentivizing positive reviews is strictly prohibited

Account Suspension

Your account may be suspended for the following violations:

  • Selling counterfeit, stolen, or prohibited items
  • Consistently failing to ship orders on time
  • High return rates due to product misrepresentation
  • Abusive behavior toward buyers or platform staff
  • Manipulating ratings or reviews
  • Multiple unresolved customer complaints
  • Violation of any applicable laws or regulations

Suspended sellers lose access to the platform. Repeat or severe violations may result in permanent account termination and forfeiture of pending payouts.

Community Standards

We are building a trusted community. As a seller, we expect you to:

  • Communicate professionally: Respond to buyer inquiries within 24 hours
  • Honor commitments: Fulfill all orders as described and within the stated timeframe
  • Be transparent: Disclose all relevant product information, including any limitations or defects
  • Resolve disputes fairly: Work toward amicable solutions before escalating
  • Report issues: Use the Content Report system to flag violations by other sellers
  • Continuous improvement: Regularly review your performance metrics and customer feedback
