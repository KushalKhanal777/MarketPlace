Policies

Prohibited & Restricted Items

Items you cannot sell or need approval to sell

Selling prohibited items results in immediate account suspension and potential legal action.

Prohibited Items

The following items are strictly prohibited from being listed, sold, or promoted on Islington Marketplace under any circumstances:

Weapons & Firearms
Guns, ammunition, explosives, knives (over legal limit), and weapon accessories
Drugs & narcotics
Illegal substances, controlled drugs, drug paraphernalia, and unregulated supplements
Counterfeit Goods
Fake branded products, replicas, and items infringing on trademarks
Stolen Goods
Items obtained through theft, fraud, or illegal means
Endangered Species
Products from protected or endangered animals and plants (ivory, exotic skins, etc.)
Recalled Items
Products officially recalled by manufacturers or regulatory authorities
Adult Content
Explicit sexual content, adult toys, pornography, and related materials
Gambling Items
Lottery tickets, casino chips, betting instruments, and gambling devices

Violations may also be reported to law enforcement authorities where applicable.

Restricted Items

The following items require prior approval from Islington Marketplace before they can be listed. Sellers must apply for approval and provide supporting documentation.

Alcohol
Alcoholic beverages (18+). Requires valid liquor license and age verification for buyers
Tobacco Products
Cigarettes, cigars, vaping products, and related accessories (18+)
Health Supplements
Dietary supplements, vitamins, herbal products — requires certification from health authorities
Electronics
Electronics require proper certification (NTA approved). Must meet safety standards

Consequences

Violations of our Prohibited and Restricted Items policy may result in:

  • First offense: Listing removal and written warning
  • Second offense: 30-day account suspension and review
  • Third offense: Permanent account termination
  • Serious violations: Immediate permanent ban and forfeiture of pending payouts
  • Illegal items: Report to law enforcement authorities and cooperation with investigations

If you are unsure whether an item is permitted, please contact us before listing.
